Privacy

  • ContextSnap runs only when you press its shortcut or click Capture. It never monitors activity or captures in the background.
  • Everything is processed on your computer. Snapshots are never sent anywhere; the app has no telemetry, accounts, or cloud features.
  • A snapshot can include visible window text, app and window names, project and Git metadata, and basic system information. Password fields are never read, and browsers contribute page content only.
  • Secrets, tokens, and email addresses are redacted locally before copying. Redaction is pattern-based and cannot catch everything, so review what you paste.
  • Nothing captured is stored. Settings are kept on your computer.
  • This website uses no cookies, analytics, or external fonts.
